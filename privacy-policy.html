Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to SmartLocker by Jenix applications, websites, dashboards, firmware-connected services, and related support systems operated by IOTSoft / Jenix ("we", "our", "us").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Name, phone, email, organization and project details shared during onboarding or support.
  • Account details submitted by admins, operators, agents, or customers.

1.2 Locker usage and device telemetry

  • Cabinet/device identifiers, board identifiers, locker IDs, lock events and audit logs.
  • Access mode metadata (such as card, PIN, face, QR, mobile app, API trigger).
  • Operational timestamps, status, and error diagnostics.

1.3 Payment-related data

  • UPI transaction references and payment status metadata required for service operation.
  • We do not store full card numbers or UPI PIN credentials.

1.4 App/device permissions

  • Depending on app features, we may process camera, Bluetooth, network, notifications, and device information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide locker automation, access control, payment flows, and operational reports.
  • To secure systems, prevent misuse, troubleshoot issues, and improve product quality.
  • To communicate updates, support responses, and critical service notifications.
  • To comply with legal, audit, and contractual obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We may share limited data with trusted service providers required for platform operation, such as:

  • Cloud hosting / infrastructure providers
  • Authentication and notification providers
  • Payment gateway partners
  • Authorized implementation partners where applicable

We do not sell personal data.

4. Data Security

We apply technical and organizational safeguards including access controls, secure transport, logging, and operational monitoring. No method of transmission or storage is fully risk-free, but we continuously improve safeguards.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • We retain data only as needed for service operation, security, legal compliance, and dispute resolution.
  • Retention periods vary by data type and contractual context.
  • You may request account/data deletion using the process in Account Deletion.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of your personal data. You may also request restriction of certain processing activities where legally applicable.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for business and institutional use and are not directed to children.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Material updates will be reflected with a new effective date.
