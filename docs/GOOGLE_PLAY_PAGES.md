## Why These Pages

Based on current Google Play policy/help guidance:

1. All apps must provide a publicly accessible privacy policy URL in Play Console and a privacy link/text in app.
2. If app supports account creation, app must offer account deletion in-app and via a web link outside the app.

## Play Console Mapping

Use these in Play Console:

1. **App content -> Privacy Policy URL**
   - `https://smartlocker.iotsoft.in/privacy-policy.html`
2. **Data safety -> Account deletion URL** (if account creation is present)
   - `https://smartlocker.iotsoft.in/account-deletion.html`
3. **Store listing / support references**
   - `https://smartlocker.iotsoft.in/app-support.html`
4. Optional company/about link
   - `https://smartlocker.iotsoft.in/about.html`
